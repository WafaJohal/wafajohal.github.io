AI LEARN is an interdisciplinary and intersectoral research and innovation centre, led by the University of Bergen (UiB) and NTNU. As part of the national investment in artificial intelligence, AI LEARN received NOK 200 million in support from the Research Council of Norway in 2025.

**Role:** International Partner (International co-PI)  
**Funding:** Research Council of Norway, 200K NOK, 4 Years  
**Duration:** 2026–2030
