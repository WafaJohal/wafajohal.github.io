The ARC Centre of Excellence for Quality Work in a Digital Age (Qwida) is composed of 7 Australian universities. The Centre's vision is to enable a future where workers and organisations are empowered by technology, rather than beholden to it.

**Role:** Chief Investigator, Proposal co-author  
**Funding:** University of Melbourne, 94M AUD  
**Duration:** 2026–2033
