This project develops user-centred tools that enhance the interaction between workers and robotic systems by adjusting the level of autonomy of the robot in response to the human's mental state (cognitive load and understanding of robot behaviour).

**Role:** Project Lead, Proposal co-author  
**Funding:** CIS & ME 2023 Research Collaboration Seed Funding, University of Melbourne, 30k AUD  
**Duration:** 2024
