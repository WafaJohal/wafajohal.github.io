The Animatas project is an EU Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Innovative Training Network (ITN ETN) that aims to explore the use of virtual agents in educational contexts.

**Role:** Proposal co-author, International Partner  
**Website:** [animatas.eu](http://animatas.eu)  
**Funding:** CHILI EPFL, 759k CHF, 3 PhD Students  
**Duration:** 2018–2022
