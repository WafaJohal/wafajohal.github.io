The project aims to help children who struggle to find their voice in social situations, by training them on joke telling using platforms such as Alexa or Google Home. It is a project funded by the SRCD that seeds cross-country and cross-discipline collaboration between researchers.

**Role:** Local Coordinator, Proposal co-author  
**Partners:** UCLIC, University of Sussex, UC Irvine  
**Funding:** CHILI EPFL, 15k CHF  
**Duration:** 2018–2020
